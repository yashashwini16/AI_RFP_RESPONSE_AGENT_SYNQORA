## Clause 3.2.1: Shariah Advisory Board Certification & AAOIFI Compliance

### RFP Requirement:
*"State whether all Islamic financial products, calculation algorithms, and contracts embedded in the proposed software are certified by an internationally accredited Shariah Supervisory Board."*

### Azentio Verified Response:
Yes. Azentio iMAL Islamic banking solutions are designed under the advisory and oversight of world-renowned Shariah scholars. 

All standard workflows, accounting entries, profit distribution algorithms, and legal contract templates undergo annual recertification verifying adherence to:
1. **AAOIFI Standards**: Full compliance with AAOIFI Shariah, Accounting, and Governance Standards.
2. **IFSB Guidelines**: Conformance with the Islamic Financial Services Board Capital Adequacy and Risk Management Framework.
3. **Local Central Bank Mandates**: Pre-configured compliance with Qatar Central Bank, Saudi Central Bank (SAMA), Central Bank of the UAE, and Bank Negara Malaysia Islamic Banking Guidelines.
