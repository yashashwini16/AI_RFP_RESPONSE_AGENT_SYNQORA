## 2.1 Immutable Shariah Audit Ledger & Non-Halal Income Purification

### Automated Purification Architecture:
In the operation of Tier-1 Islamic financial institutions, inadvertent interest accrual from cross-border correspondent accounts or operational non-compliance must be strictly purged.

### Key Capabilities:
1. **Automated Detection & Segregation**:
   - Any transaction flagging non-compliant profit recognition is halted before general ledger consolidation.
   - Non-compliant earnings are automatically diverted to a designated **Purification / Charity Suspense Ledger** (Account 2980-CHARITY).
2. **Immutable Shariah Audit Trail**:
   - Every product parameter modification, fatwa attachment, and transaction exception is recorded in a WORM-compliant audit trail.
   - Generates annual Shariah Supervisory Board (SSB) audit reports directly matching Central Bank regulatory reporting templates.
